Event Tamizha — account and data deletion

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open the Event Tamizha app and sign in.
  2. Tap Profile (bottom tab).
  3. Tap Delete my account, enter your password and tap Yes, delete.

Deletion is immediate.

If you cannot sign in

Send us a request from the mobile number registered on the account:

  • WhatsApp or call +91 95667 79289, or
  • e-mail giri.kalingiyam@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Event Tamizha account” and your registered mobile number.

We verify that the request comes from the account owner and delete the account within 30 days (usually within 3 days).

What is deleted

  • Your login, name, saved address and location, notifications and sessions.
  • In finished or cancelled bookings: your name, mobile number, venue address, map position, review text, and the photos you uploaded.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Booking amounts and dates (without your personal details) for business and tax records.
  • Bookings still in progress keep the contact details needed to finish the service; they are anonymised afterwards on request.
  • Information we must keep by law.

Questions? See the Privacy Policy.
