This policy explains what information the Event Tamizha website and Android app collect, why, who sees it, and the choices you have. We do not sell your data and we show no advertising.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, mobile number and password. The password is stored only as a salted hash, never as plain text.
  • Booking details: function type and date, town, venue address, number of guests, notes, the pandal, items and size you choose, the estimated price.
  • Location (optional): your saved location and the venue location, as GPS coordinates or a map link you paste. We ask for the phone’s location permission only when you tap “Enable location”. Providers must share a base location so we can give them nearby jobs.
  • Photos (optional): pictures you attach to a booking or a review.
  • Notifications and device token: messages about your bookings, and your choice whether to allow phone alerts. If you allow notifications in the Android app, Google Firebase Cloud Messaging gives your phone a device token (a random code that identifies the app on your phone). We store this token linked to your mobile number so we can send you booking, job and status alerts, even when the app is closed. We remove it when you sign out or delete your account.
  • Technical data: your language choice and items in your cart are kept on your device. Our web hosting and Google services may log your IP address and device type as part of normal operation.

We do not use advertising or analytics tools and we do not collect your contacts, SMS, call logs, microphone or camera feed. The camera or photo picker opens only when you tap “Camera”, “Gallery” or “Photo”, and only the pictures you choose are uploaded.

3. Why we use it

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To receive, price, schedule and carry out your booking, and to contact you about it (call, WhatsApp, in-app notification).
  • To choose a suitable provider near the venue.
  • To send you status updates and, if you allow, phone alerts.
  • To keep business and accounting records, prevent misuse and answer complaints.

4. Who can see it

  • Event Tamizha team and admin: all booking details.
  • The service provider assigned to your booking: your name, mobile number, function details, venue address and map position, notes and photos for that booking only.
  • Our technology provider, Google: bookings, accounts and photos are stored in Google Sheets, Google Drive and Google Apps Script under our Google account, e-mail notices go through Gmail, and phone alerts are delivered through Firebase Cloud Messaging (the alert title and text pass through Google’s servers to reach your phone). Google processes this data for us under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Public reviews: if you leave a review, your first name, rating, comment and review photos may appear on our website and App.
  • We may disclose information if the law requires it. We do not sell or rent personal data.

5. Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed. Access to the data sheets is limited to the owner. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you do not use elsewhere.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account data: until you delete your account.
  • Device token: until you sign out on that phone, delete your account, or the token stops working (we then delete it automatically).
  • Finished or cancelled bookings: when you delete your account we remove your name, mobile number, address, map position, review text and photos from them; the amounts and dates stay as business records.
  • Bookings still in progress when you delete your account keep their contact details until the service is finished.

7. Your choices and rights

  • See and correct: Profile → Edit profile (name, number, address, location).
  • Delete: Profile → Delete my account, or follow these steps if you cannot sign in.
  • Location, photos, camera, notifications: optional. You can refuse or turn them off in your phone’s settings at any time (Settings → Apps → Event Tamizha → Permissions / Notifications); the App still works for browsing and estimates.
  • You may also ask us in writing to give you a copy of your data or to correct or remove it.

8. Children

The App is for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the App.

10. Contact / grievance

For any privacy question, request or complaint, contact us using the details in section 1. We will reply within 7 days.
